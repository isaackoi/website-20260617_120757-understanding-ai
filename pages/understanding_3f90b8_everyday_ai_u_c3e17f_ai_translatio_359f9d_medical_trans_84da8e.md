## Introduction

AI-powered translation can help patients and clinicians communicate when they do not share a common language, reducing delays and expanding access to care. In many situations, modern machine translation systems can accurately translate routine health information, appointment details, and standard written instructions. However, [healthcare]({{ 'health-proxy/' | relative_url }}) is a high-stakes environment in which small wording errors can have serious consequences. A mistranslated dosage, symptom description, allergy warning, or consent form may affect diagnosis, treatment, or a patient's [understanding]({{ 'understanding/' | relative_url }}) of medical risks. The central question is therefore not whether AI translation works, but when it is accurate enough to support care and when human review remains essential. Recent studies suggest that AI translation is improving rapidly, yet clinically significant errors continue to occur, particularly in complex or sensitive communications.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12252260/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PMC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Evaluation of the accuracy and safety of machine translation of...by M Kong · 2025 · Cited by 32 — Recommended safety precautions inc...</span></span></span>

## How Accurate Is Medical AI Translation Today?

Evidence from recent research shows a mixed picture. Modern neural translation systems often perform well on common language pairs and routine medical text, but performance varies substantially by language, context, and task.

A 2024 systematic review examining AI-assisted clinical translation found generally positive results for translation accuracy and usability, while also noting that evidence for direct patient-clinician communication remains limited and requires further evaluation. Researchers concluded that language barriers can be reduced through AI tools, but accuracy and safety concerns remain important.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39817236/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PubMed]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">a systematic review of its role in language translation and...by A Genovese · 2024 · Cited by 85 — This review evaluates AI&#x27;s effe...</span></span></span>

More detailed studies reveal why caution is needed. A comparative analysis of AI-translated discharge instructions found that systems such as ChatGPT-4 and Google Translate could generate useful translations, yet some outputs contained errors with potential clinical consequences. Researchers recommended avoiding machine translation for high-stakes communication and emphasised verbal review of translated instructions with patients whenever possible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12252260/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PMC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Evaluation of the accuracy and safety of machine translation of...by M Kong · 2025 · Cited by 32 — Recommended safety precautions inc...</span></span></span>

Recent evaluations have also shown that performance can differ dramatically between languages. In one study comparing AI-generated translations with professional translations of real discharge instructions, results were broadly comparable for Spanish but showed greater shortcomings in other languages, with clinically important errors still occurring.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2839035" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jamanetwork.com">[JAMA Network]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jamanetwork.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">JAMA NetworkAccuracy of AI vs Professionally Translated Discharge...September 17, 2025 — by M Martos · 2025 · Cited by 15 — The results...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 17, 2025</span></span></span>

## Medication and Dosage Instructions

Medication instructions are among the most sensitive forms of medical communication because accuracy must extend beyond general meaning to precise details.

Patients need to understand:

* How much medicine to take.
* How often to take it.
* When to stop or continue treatment.
* What side effects require [attention]({{ 'attention/' | relative_url }}).
* Which medicines should not be combined.

Even a seemingly minor translation error can change the intended treatment. Researchers studying machine translation of patient-specific discharge instructions have identified mistakes involving medication directions, timing, and clinical terminology that could potentially lead to harm if followed literally.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12252260/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PMC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Evaluation of the accuracy and safety of machine translation of...by M Kong · 2025 · Cited by 32 — Recommended safety precautions inc...</span></span></span>

Studies examining healthcare translation have therefore recommended simplifying source-language instructions before translation. Clear, plain-language writing reduces ambiguity and improves machine translation performance. Researchers have also explored [automated]({{ 'decisions/' | relative_url }}) methods for converting complex prescription language into patient-friendly instructions, showing that AI can sometimes improve comprehension when carefully designed and validated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://facctconference.org/static/pdfs_2022/facct22-3533244.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: facctconference.org">[FAccT Conference]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">facctconference.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">facct22 3533244</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Due to the risk of.Read more...</span></span></span>

The practical implication is that AI translation can support medication communication, but clinicians should not assume that a fluent-sounding translation is necessarily safe. [Verification]({{ 'verification-cost/' | relative_url }}) remains important whenever dosage or treatment instructions are involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12252260/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PMC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Evaluation of the accuracy and safety of machine translation of...by M Kong · 2025 · Cited by 32 — Recommended safety precautions inc...</span></span></span>

## Why Clinically Significant Errors Still Happen

Modern translation systems are dramatically better than earlier word-for-word approaches, yet several risks remain.

**Medical terminology can be ambiguous.** Some terms have different meanings depending on context, specialty, or country. A translation that appears grammatically correct may still convey the wrong clinical meaning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare-translation-a-contemporary-/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: researchers.mq.edu.au">[Macquarie University]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">researchers.mq.edu.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">artificial intelligence in healthcare translation a contemporary</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Macquarie UniversityArtificial intelligence in healthcare translationby VE Raído · 2025 · Cited by 7 — This systematic review elaborates...</span></span></span>

**Rare languages and dialects are often underrepresented.** AI systems generally perform best on languages with large training datasets. Researchers have highlighted concerns about reduced performance for Indigenous languages, regional dialects, and less commonly translated language pairs.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12875660/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PMC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">PMCEthical Risks and Structural Implications of AI-Mediated</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">by AL Vera · 2026 · Cited by 1 — In light of these concerns, uncritical adoption of AI interpreting poses ethical and structural risks...</span></span></span>

Translating isolated sentences may produce outputs that appear correct while missing important contextual meaning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39817236/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PubMed]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">a systematic review of its role in language translation and...by A Genovese · 2024 · Cited by 85 — This review evaluates AI&#x27;s effe...</span></span></span>

**[Fluency]({{ 'fluency-vs-accuracy/' | relative_url }}) can hide mistakes.** One of the strengths of modern AI is generating natural-sounding text. Ironically, this can make errors harder to detect because incorrect translations often appear polished and confident. ## Expert Review for High-Stakes Decisions

The strongest evidence emerging from current research is not that AI translation should be rejected, but that its use should be matched to the level of risk.

Low-risk communications such as appointment reminders, basic educational materials, and routine administrative information may benefit substantially from AI translation because the consequences of occasional imperfections are limited.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39817236/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PubMed]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">a systematic review of its role in language translation and...by A Genovese · 2024 · Cited by 85 — This review evaluates AI&#x27;s effe...</span></span></span>

Higher-risk situations generally warrant human review, including:

* Medication and dosage instructions.

* Surgical or procedural consent.
* Discussions of serious diagnoses.
* End-of-life conversations.
* Mental health assessments.
* Complex treatment planning.

Recent healthcare studies recommend combining machine translation with human [oversight]({{ 'oversight/' | relative_url }}) rather than treating the technology as a replacement for professional interpreters or translators. Suggested safeguards include reviewing translated instructions verbally, retaining original-language text, simplifying source documents, and involving trained language professionals when decisions carry significant clinical consequences.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12252260/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov">[PMC+2Nature]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Evaluation of the accuracy and safety of machine translation of...by M Kong · 2025 · Cited by 32 — Recommended safety precautions inc...</span></span></span>
